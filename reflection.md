# Reflection

### 1. What I got wrong at the start
When I began this challenge, I thought that the real problem was student inconvenience. I thought students had to walk 100 meters to guard room judt to write the complaint, so my first thought was to design a full-stack application where students can submit the complaints.
But when I met with Supervisor sir then i was able to understand that the real problem is not that the complaints are not solved because it is not mentioned properly.But He explained that electricians, plumbers, and carpenters are hired from outside and charge for every visit. So, technician is called  when there is atleast 4–5 complaints.
Even after the technician arrives, there are more problems. They often waste time going between different floors, ans sometime even students are also not present in their rooms.
I realized that just creating an app to collect complaints wouldn't solve the problem. The real challenge was managing complaints, knowing when to call a technician, and grouping tasks floor-wise to save workers' time.

### 2. What I would cut
If I had only two hours, I would focus on one department instead of handling all four. I would also remove the regex-based time preference feature because student schedules keep changing, making it difficult to manage. Sending WhatsApp messages so students can coordinate their availability would be a simpler and more practical solution.

### 3. Where AI helped
AI helped me in three main ways. First, it converted 74 messy handwritten entries from register photos into a clean CSV file, saving a lot of manual work. Second, it helped me write regex logic to handle different room number formats. Third, it helped me improve the project idea by pointing out that my initial approach was becoming just a basic to-do app rather than solving the actual problem.

### 4. Where AI wasted time
AI sometimes wasted time by suggesting unnecessary features like database schemas, extra libraries, and complex complaint-priority formulas. These were not needed for a simple 4-hour project. I also had to manually check date formats because AI struggled with inconsistent handwritten dates. Overall, AI helped speed up development, but I had to keep it focused on the actual problem.
