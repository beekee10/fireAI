**Perplexity AI Response & Sourced References:**

### Source 1: IITRAM Complaint Guidelines
- **Title:** Guidelines for Students’ Complaints and Redressal Mechanism
- **Institution:** Indian Institute of Technology (IITRAM), Ahmedabad
- **Working Link:** https://iitram.ac.in/public/upload/20240503_IITRAM_Guidelines%20for%20Complaints%20and%20their%20Redressal%20v1Mechanismv2.pdf
- **Summary & Relevance:**
  1. Documents that hostel students report maintenance complaints in a physical complaint register kept at the Hostel/Mess office, exactly matching our manual system.
  2. The caretaker inspects the register daily and sorts complaints across separate trades (civil, water works, electrical, sanitary, network).
  3. Confirms that caretakers must follow up on complaints for early resolution, validating the need for an automated triage and dispatch tool.

### Source 2: TISS Hostel Complaint System
- **Title:** Complaints Management Systems
- **Institution:** Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), Mumbai
- **Working Link:** https://www.tiss.ac.in/uploads/files/Complaints_Management_Systems.pdf
- **Summary & Relevance:**
  1. Analyzes a structured institutional complaints system categorizing issues across electrical, plumbing, and civil maintenance sections.
  2. Demonstrates the operational value of categorized complaint routing and structured assignment to maintenance teams.
  3. Addresses the problem where informal verbal or phone complaints get buried and forgotten without centralized records.
